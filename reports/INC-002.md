## 5. Email Content Analysis

The email claim that 2FA will become mandatory for all MetaMask user and ask them to enable it before the deadline. It also warn that access to certain wallet features will be restricted if users fail to complete the process.

**Suspicious observations:**
- Use MetaMask branding to appear legitimate.
- Creates urgency through a mandatory 2FA notice.
- Threatens restricted wallet access.
- Uses a Firebase hosted sender address.

**Deadline mismatch:**

- Subject: 31/12/2025
- Email body: 31/03/2026

The different deadlines are another suspicious element in the email.

## 7. HTML Content Analysis

The HTML body is designed like an official MetaMask security notification. It contain the MetaMask logo, an orange 2FA banner, an action required label, a deadline section and an "Enable 2FA Now" button.

The design appears intended to make the email look like a genuine security announcement.

## 10. MITRE ATT&CK Mapping

## 11. Recommended Mitigation
1. Investigate the SendGrid URL in a safe environment.
2. Identify and check its final destination using URLScan and VirusTotal.
3. Search mailboxes for similar messages and sender addresses.
4. Quarantine matching emails and block confirmed malicious URLs.
5. If users submitted sensitive wallet information, treat their wallets as potentially compromised.
6. Monitor for similar MetaMask impersonation attempts.

## 12. Analyst Conclusion
The email shows several sign of MetaMask impersonation phishing. It use a Firebase hosted sender address, creates urgency through a mandatory 2FA notice and directs users to an external tracking link.

SPF and DKIM passed, but this only confirms authentication for the sending domain, not its affiliation with MetaMask. The different deadlines in the subject and body also raise suspicion.

**Final Assessment:** Likely malicious: MetaMask impersonation phishing.

**Pending:** Further investigation is required to identify the final destination of the SendGrid tracking URL and confirm its maliciousness.
